Downloaded 2026-09-18 from Wikimedia Commons.

## monge.jpg
- File: `Gaspard_monge_litho_delpech.jpg`
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gaspard_monge_litho_delpech.jpg
- Lithograph of Gaspard Monge by Francois-Seraphin Delpech (1778-1825).
- 898 x 968. Ink-on-white artwork -- use `invert0 = true` so the ink carries
  the mass; the raw intensity would put it all in the white background.
- **Licence: public domain.** The work and its author both predate 1900, so it
  is PD by age in every relevant jurisdiction. Safe to publish, no attribution
  legally required (crediting Delpech is still good practice).

## kantorovich.jpg
- File: `Leonid_Kantorovich_1975.jpg`
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Leonid_Kantorovich_1975.jpg
- Photograph of Leonid Kantorovich, 1975.
- 520 x 670. Bright subject on a mid-grey ground -- `invert1 = false`.
- **Licence: dual GFDL / CC-BY-3.0. Photographer: Andrei Bogdanoff.**
- ATTRIBUTION IS REQUIRED. This is a free licence, so it may be used in a
  paper, including commercially -- but the credit must appear. A figure
  caption should carry something like:

      Photograph of L. V. Kantorovich (1975) by Andrei Bogdanoff,
      CC BY 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

  Do not drop this when the figure is copied into a draft: CC-BY without the
  BY is a licence violation, not a citation-style preference.
